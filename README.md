# 🎬 Netflix Content Analysis | Tableau

## 📖 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **Netflix Content Analysis** using **Tableau Public**. The objective was to analyze Netflix's global content library, identify content distribution patterns, explore genre preferences, and uncover trends in content production across different countries and years.

The interactive dashboard transforms raw data into meaningful visualizations, enabling users to explore key performance indicators and gain valuable business insights through data storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

As one of the world's leading streaming platforms, Netflix continuously expands its content library to meet the diverse preferences of a global audience. Understanding content distribution, genre popularity, geographical availability, and release trends is essential for making informed content acquisition and production decisions.

This project aims to analyze Netflix's content catalog and provide actionable insights that support strategic business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
- Explore genre popularity across the Netflix catalog.
- Identify countries with the largest content libraries.
- Analyze yearly content release trends.
- Build an interactive dashboard for data-driven storytelling.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- **Movies** account for approximately **68%** of Netflix's content library, while **TV Shows** represent the remaining **32%**, indicating a stronger focus on film-based content.
- **Documentaries** and **Stand-Up Comedy** are among the most prominent genres, reflecting significant viewer demand for educational and entertainment-focused content.
- The **United States** hosts the largest Netflix content library, contributing the highest number of both Movies and TV Shows.
- **2020** recorded the highest number of new content releases, demonstrating Netflix's significant investment in expanding its catalog during that period.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies are recommended:

- Continue expanding the content library with a balanced mix of Movies and TV Shows to meet diverse audience preferences.
- Increase investment in high-performing genres such as Documentaries and Stand-Up Comedy while exploring emerging content categories.
- Strengthen collaborations with international content creators to diversify regional offerings and enhance global audience engagement.
- Utilize historical release trends and audience preferences to optimize future content acquisition and production strategies.
